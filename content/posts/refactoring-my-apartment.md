This Sunday I took the time to declutter my apartment and put items where they are supposed to be stored. I consider this as a form of meditation. The hard part is gaining momentum (it is a Sunday after all) and keeping the ball rolling (don't sit down to take a break or you're done!). Once the apartment looks tidy again I feel my balance is restored too and I am able to pick up my projects again with more clarity and calmness.
